# Job Hunt Second Brain - Structure

## Folders
- **Research** — market scans, interview trends, company briefs
- **Skills** — knowledge profile, strengths, gaps, analogies
- **Interview Logs** — transcripts, Whisper summaries, TODO follow-ups
- **Projects** — practice builds, repo pointers, lessons learned
- **Meta** — templates, agent configs, operating notes

## Note Naming
- Research: `YYYY-MM-DD - Topic.md`
- Skill entries: `Skill - Topic.md`
- Interview logs: `YYYY-MM-DD - Interview - Topic.md`
- Projects: `Project - Name.md`
